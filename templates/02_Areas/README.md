# 02_Areas

Ongoing responsibilities — the parts of life and work you maintain but never "finish." Unlike a project, an area has no end date: health, finances, your team, the house. If you'd feel the drop if you ignored it for a month, it's probably an area.

**Example:** `02_Areas/finances/` holding a running note of subscriptions and renewal dates.

**Try this — say to Claude:** "What areas of responsibility do I seem to have, based on my notes so far? Suggest two or three folders for this Areas section."

Rule of thumb: projects end, areas don't.
